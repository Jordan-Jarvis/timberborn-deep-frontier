## Art and materials

Use the exact installed Iron Teeth wood/plank, blue painted-metal roof, base-metal, forged-panel, orange infill and white-plaster materials. Oversized wood grain is intentional. Civic architecture has 30 material slots; native prop meshes keep their original slots, normals, UV channels and vertex colors. Do not recolor or rescale the whole building to fit an old footprint.

Other plants use their base foliage. Custom flowers are permitted if they use equally convincing leaf/petal geometry and the same visual style.

Available optional foods and coffee are included once per meal. Guests remain seated for another 0.1 game hours. Visits and active service executors save through native persistence. ## Hospital treatment contracts inspected in 1.1.2.8

The native Medical Bed authors Injury +0.015 per active treatment hour. `Need.Update` applies passive +0.1/day only on ticks without an applied effect. The first attraction visit lasts 0.5 hours, followed by repeated 0.05-hour visits. In the 1.1.2.8 fixture, continued care alternates treatment and executor-completion/passive ticks: build 62 measured **0.009583455/hour** after settling. This matches `(0.015 + 0.1/24)/2`. For the same native cadence, Hospital authors +0.0303333333 on treatment ticks, giving a steady target `(0.0303333333 + 0.1/24)/2 = 0.01725/hour`, or 1.8× the measured bed rate. Passive healing is not added on an active treatment tick. The target concerns continued treatment; the initial half-hour burst and travel are different. Build 63 measured 0.01672733/hour in a short 1.03125-hour sample, a 1.745439 ratio within the declared ±0.08 tolerance. Build 66 measured three-staff recovery at 0.009666424/hour.

The native Teeth Grindstone supplies ChippedTeeth +0.5/hour and satisfies to its maximum. The Decontamination Pod supplies BadwaterContamination +0.068/hour; its native powered consumable is Extract at 0.1/hour with a 100-hour buffer. These are independent needs and must remain separate treatment effects. All three native buildings use `AttractionSpec` and a limited `EnterableSpec`. A hospital can share one 30-patient admission limit, but staff must not consume its patient slots.

Use the public `RangedEffectBuilding.AddEffect`/`RemoveEffect` methods for the neighborhood effect. Native range propagation already handles pause/blocking and mechanical efficiency; it does not intrinsically require stock or staff. The authored Hospital adapter attaches its effect only while actual staff are at the ward work point, shaft power is available and native supplies remain. Exact evidence: the three installed Wellbeing blueprints, the corresponding native Need blueprints and `outputs/civic-native-api/Timberborn.RangedEffectSystem.cs`. Build 66 verified actual inside-radius gain, outside decay, medicine exhaustion and replenishment.
