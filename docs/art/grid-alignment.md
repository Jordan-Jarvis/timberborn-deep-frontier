# Building edges and platform decks

Target: Timberborn 1.1.2.8, Iron Teeth. User decision on 2026-10-20: players place native paths on the decks, as on vanilla platforms. Internal door-to-door connections stay inside the building. This supersedes the older built-in outdoor-road design.

The visible floor boundaries, rooms, placement cells and picking volumes must be authored together. Do not fit a fractional mesh into larger cell boxes after export. Structural bounds use native grid XYZ with exclusive upper edges; doors face a whole-cell boundary and are centered on the adjoining tile. Ornament, roof slopes, window frames and individual timber details retain fractional dimensions.

The restaurant and revised two-wing hospital use explicit structural declarations in `restaurant_structure.py` and `hospital_structure.py`. `grid_structure.py` creates those parts and emits their exact picking boxes. `tools/grid_structure.mjs` consumes the same declarations for native occupation. Room walls accept shafts at exposed horizontal faces on multiple levels; actual door openings are excluded. Native `Corners` is reserved only at explicitly authored post cells, with `Top, Corners` in the slab layer. Clear bays remain empty. Only physical clear deck surfaces provide stacking support. Fixed dining furniture reserves its cells; the restaurant's white roof terrace stays clear for a native 3×3 Rooftop Terrace.

The user rejected the first migration's dense support grid and ground-reaching rooftop legs. The revised restaurant has sixteen short posts at structural junctions, continuous edge beams and no posts beneath the cantilevered outer roof terrace. Its current source has 115 picking boxes and 59 rail openings, including the rear balcony and vestibule cap; earlier counts below describe historical packages. Closed-room plaster and exposed wood occupy separate wall sections, with infill recessed behind the timber frame. The full evaluated overlap audit and separate shell/window-contact checks cover defects beyond the original narrow plaster-face test.

`PlatformDeckSpec` supplies rail openings and interior door links. The reusable `PlatformDeck` component uses native navigation notifications and model-node visibility. It requires a finished native Path/Stairs edge on **both sides** before opening the corresponding rail section. Outdoor road edges belong to those Path entities. This removes the need to find a neighboring building component in an otherwise empty floor cell, and aligned slabs require no connection-only filler planks. Internal links use the native building navigation specification. There is no alternate lookup, mesh scaling, path replacement or Harmony patch.

Native platform evidence: `Buildings/Paths/DoublePlatform/DoublePlatform.IronTeeth.blueprint.json` uses lower `Corners`, upper `Top, Corners`, `Stackable: BlockObject` and `GenerateFloorsOnStackable: true`. Native `Path` separately reserves `Path`. `NavMeshObjectUpdater` creates ordinary floor edges above stackable cells; player roads provide road edges. Placement and picking remain separate native contracts.

## Native entrance on the path cell (2026-10-21)

The user reported that player paths reached the Restaurant and Materials Laboratory doors without drawing the native connection stub. Timberborn 1.1.2.8 `DynamicPathModel` picks its connected model from `ConnectionService.CanConnect`. The entrance branch only passes when the native entrance flag sits on the path cell itself: `PositionedEntrance.Coordinates` is the path cell, and `DoorstepCoordinates` is the door cell one step inside. Evidence: `outputs/navigation-api-1.1.2.8/Timberborn.PathSystem.decompiled.cs` and `Timberborn.BlockSystem.decompiled.cs`. 138 of 139 vanilla entrances use `Y: -1`.

Our generators had put `Entrance` on the door cell itself. `civic_building_content.mjs` and `hospital_building_content.mjs` now derive it from the ground door link's exterior cell, with `nativeEntrance()` failing if that cell isn't one horizontal step out or isn't free. `research_building_content.mjs` uses the cell in front of the ground door. Regeneration changed exactly three values: Restaurant (2,2,0)→(2,1,0), Hospital (7,2,0)→(7,1,0), Materials Laboratory (1,0,0)→(1,-1,0). `validate_vertical_slice_content.mjs` and `validate_restaurant_grid.mjs` pass. The upper doors keep using the `PlatformDeck`/`BuildingWalkway` connection override, since a building has only one native entrance. The in-game stub, worker access and save/reload are not yet verified.

## Crusher, kiln and mixer grid registration (2026-10-21, source/export only)

User request: line the factory buildings' edges and heights up with the tiles, keeping the approved look. The three scenes were rebuilt from source (`render_reference_studies.py --models StoneCrusher,CementKiln,ConcreteMixer --save-only`; unchanged sources first reproduced the saved scenes exactly) and exported with `export_industrial_models.py`. Registration still uses `BuiltInWalkways`.

- **Origins:** crusher `[1.3,1.71]` → `[0,0]`; kiln `[1,2]` and mixer `[0,0]` unchanged. New crusher structure is authored in grid XY. Its reviewed machinery, hopper, roofs and fittings are placed rigidly by `factory_registration.RigidPlacement`, which moves vertices and animation pivots together without scaling.
- **Edges:** lane decks, outer walkway posts and pier footings now sit on the tile edges. Room walls that face open bays are also on edges: the crusher's at x=1, x=5 and y=6, the kiln control room at x=4..5, and the mixer operator room at x≈0 and y=4. Rails are one run per cell (`cell_rails`), 0.08 inside the edge, with posts on cell boundaries. Corner posts sit 0.08 inside the corner cell, so the exporter removes them along with that cell's corner return.
- **Doors and entrances:** each door is centred on a tile, and its face is 0.10, 0.14 or 0.12 from the tile edge. `industrial_layouts.ground_door()` registers the door, and `industrial_building_content.mjs checkGroundDoor()` enforces it: the doorstep must be occupied, the entrance must be the free path cell in front and the idle slot 0.08 in front of the face. The mixer Entrance moves from the door cell (1,0,0) to (1,-1,0). The kiln stair access moves to column 2, and the front openings over the porch and output bin (x 3..6) were removed.
- **Occupancy:** `industrial_occupancy.mjs` trio branches follow the art. The alignment audit on the new scenes finds no empty `All` cells and no substantial visible art in `None` cells. The remaining findings are edge slivers and corner posts/footings wider than the analyzer's 0.2 corner zone.
- **Shaft faces:** `industrial_wall_faces.py` measures wall faces on the saved scene with the same rule as `measure_wall_faces.py` and writes `outputs/industrial-integration/<Model>-wall-faces.json` with the scene SHA-256. The content generator refuses a stale list. Faces toward empty cells come from `solidPowerFaces(..., {walls})`. Faces toward open bays under the walks (Corners / Top+Corners) are passed as kept sockets. Result: crusher 20, kiln 3, mixer 10 horizontal faces, all on walls. Before, crusher, kiln and mixer had 59, 85 and 120, nearly all in air.
- **Overlap audit:** the default-epsilon gate shows 0 visible conflicts for all three. At epsilon 0.004 the counts are crusher 144, kiln 534, mixer 133. The pre-change baseline at 0.004 was 145, 572 and 176, so these are pre-existing machinery details such as kiln barrel plates, mixer drum bands, roof wear chips and casks.

Not yet run: canonical generation, promotion, build and any in-game check. Evidence is in `outputs/alignment-audit/trio-after/`.

## Deck-free industrial machines (2026-10-21, source/export only)

User request: remove the balconies from the machines. The crusher, kiln, mixer and both quarries (one `MineralQuarry` scene) no longer have raised walkable decks, balconies or railed walkways. Nobody walks on them above the ground door. Registration is now `deckMode: 'None'` with no lanes, rail openings or ports. The blueprints lose `BuildingWalkwaySpec` and `BlockObjectNavMeshSettingsSpec`, and `industrial_building_content.mjs` throws if a layout brings any of them back.

- **Structure instead of decks:** the machines keep their height. Former walkway runs became concrete caps and plinths (`factory_registration.machine_slab`), timber hall frames, trestles, piers and enclosed housings. Crusher: a concrete housing roof over the workshop at deck level. Kiln: a concrete cap over the one-tile control room; the drum and feed bins stand on the timber hall frame and the masonry firebox. Mixer: the operator room has a concrete roof; the perimeter piers, edge beams, rails and storage deck are gone; the feed tower, cement hopper and header-tank tower stand on their own piers. Quarry: a solid stone process plinth (top at layer 1) in the same stepped footprint, and a maintenance ladder mast with one narrow, rail-free hatch landing at 7. The galleries, apron ladder and loading gate are gone.
- **Doors:** unchanged from the grid pass. Quarry ground cabin door: centred on grid x=1.5, face 0.14 behind the y=2 tile edge, Entrance (1,1,0). The quarry's upper doors (LeftWorkshopDoor, RightWorkshopDoor, ControlRoomDoor) are decorative and off-grid; they lead nowhere walkable.
- **Occupancy:** `industrial_occupancy.mjs` follows the new art (former deck cells are solid or empty as the art dictates). The quarry keeps its 25 forced `Top` seam cells.
- **Shaft faces:** re-measured on the new scenes with `industrial_wall_faces.py` (now also for `MineralQuarry`). See the counts below.
- **Mixer pipe and power line:** water inlet at (6,4,0) facing Right, at the foot of the header-tank tower; power line connection at grid (6.9,4.9,3.8).
- **Validators:** `validate_industrial_exports.py` asserts no walkway spec, no NavMesh spec, no rail or WalkwayJoin nodes. `validate_construction_materials.mjs` expects the mixer port at (6,4,0) Right. `render_reference_motion.py` no longer runs walk checks for the crusher and kiln. `IndustrialAcceptance.cs` keeps the solid-room, power, production, traversal and save/reload checks; deck stairs, platforms, rail ports and deck routes are gone. Routes and walks now target the ground-door Entrance cells, and the shaft is built on the first transput's first horizontal face. `MixerAcceptance.cs` drops its stairs, deck platforms, under-deck tests, rail ports and deck waypoints.

Art in `None` cells is not visible and is almost all edge slivers. The quarry's 25 forced `Top` seam cells still hold the collar and plinth, which is inherent to the seam contract.
